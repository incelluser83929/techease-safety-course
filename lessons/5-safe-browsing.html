Safe Browsing & Downloads

Avoid the fake pop-ups and downloads that install malware.

Only download from trusted sources

  • Get apps only from your device's official app store (App Store, Google Play, Microsoft Store)
  • Download software only from the company's real, official website
  • Be extra cautious with "free" versions of paid software, movies, or games — these are common malware traps

Watch out for fake pop-ups

A pop-up claiming "Your Flash Player is out of date" or "Update your browser now" is almost always fake — and clicking it can install malware, including ransomware. Real software updates come from within the app itself or your device's settings, not a random pop-up while browsing.

⚠️ Never click:

  • "You've won a prize!" pop-ups
  • "Virus detected — click here to remove" banner ads
  • Countdown timers pressuring you to act immediately

Public Wi-Fi caution

Public Wi-Fi at a coffee shop, airport, or library isn't always secure. Avoid entering passwords or financial information while connected to public Wi-Fi unless you trust the network, or use your phone's cellular data instead for anything sensitive.

Check before you click "sensitive"

Before entering a password or credit card number, check that the website address starts with https:// (not just http://) and look for a small lock icon in your browser's address bar. This means the connection is encrypted — though it doesn't guarantee the site itself is trustworthy, so it's still worth double-checking the web address is spelled correctly.

✅ Quick tips

  • Download apps and software only from official sources
  • Never click "update now" pop-ups that appear while browsing
  • Avoid entering sensitive information on public Wi-Fi
  • Double check web addresses before entering passwords or payment details
