Plain-language glossary

Common terms, explained simply

Malware
Short for "malicious software" — any program designed to harm your device or steal information. Ransomware and viruses are both types of malware.
Ransomware
Malware that locks or encrypts your files and demands payment to restore access.
Phishing
A fake email, text, or call that pretends to be from a trusted source to trick you into sharing information or clicking a link.
Two-factor authentication (2FA)
A second login step — usually a code sent to your phone — that protects your account even if someone steals your password.
Password manager
An app that creates and remembers strong, unique passwords for every website, so you only need to remember one master password.
Backup
A copy of your files stored somewhere separate from your main device, so you can recover them if something goes wrong.
VPN (Virtual Private Network)
A tool that encrypts your internet connection, adding a layer of privacy — especially useful on public Wi-Fi.
Two-step verification
Another common name for two-factor authentication (2FA).
